# 2027 Couples Trip — Option Menu

**Group:** You + Tristan, plus 2–3 other couples (late 20s)
**Departure cities:** Toronto, New York, Boston — all three fly direct to everywhere below, so no one's stuck with a connection.
**Pricing:** Per couple, mid-range room/cabin, in **USD** ([≈ 1 USD = 1.41 CAD](https://wise.com/gb/currency-converter/usd-to-cad-rate/history) as of Oct 2026, fluctuates — the live planner site has a USD/CAD toggle).

✈️ **Heads up — flights aren't included.** Every price below is lodging/cabin only. Tack on roughly **$400–900/couple round-trip** from YYZ/JFK/BOS (more like $700–1,100 for Lisbon's longer flight) — though depending where everyone's coming from, the NYC cruise could end up flight-free.

⚠️ **Reality check on these numbers:** it's already Oct 2026, and resorts/cruises usually open booking 12–18 months out, so most of 2027 is already bookable or close to it. These prices are based on real comparable listings, not guesses — but still treat them as a ballpark for the budget poll, not a locked-in quote. Get the real number once the group's down to 1-2 favorites.

## 🏖️ Resort (all-inclusive, one home base, easiest to keep a group together)

### 1. Riviera Maya / Tulum, Mexico
- **Vibe:** Swim-up bars, beach clubs, cenote day trips, and genuinely good food if you venture off-resort
- **Price:** ~$1,800–2,500/couple for 5 nights all-in
- **Pros:** Shortest flight (4.5–5.5 hrs), huge resort selection so budget and splurge couples can book different room tiers at the same resort, easy Spanish-adjacent service
- **Cons:** Cancun airport transfer traffic, occasional cartel headlines that spook people more than the actual risk in resort zones, hot and humid July–Sept
- **Best time:** Mid-December through April — skip Aug–Oct, peak hurricane season here
- **Book:** [All-inclusive hotels in Tulum](https://www.expedia.com/Tulum-Hotels-All-Inclusive-Hotel.0-0-d182189-tAllinclusiveHotel.Travel-Guide-Filter-Hotels) (Expedia)

### 2. Punta Cana, Dominican Republic
- **Price:** ~$1,500–2,200/couple for 5 nights all-in
- **Pros:** Cheapest all-in resort option here, huge swim-up-bar mega-resorts built for groups, direct flights from all 3 cities
- **Cons:** Less to do off-resort than Mexico, beach erosion in some areas
- **Best time:** December–April is prime; June–Nov is cheaper but bring solid travel insurance for hurricane season
- **Book:** [Punta Cana vacation deals & all-inclusive resorts](https://www.cheapcaribbean.com/destinations/dominican-republic/punta-cana/) (CheapCaribbean)

### 3. Montego Bay / Negril, Jamaica
- **Price:** ~$2,000–2,800/couple for 5 nights all-in
- **Pros:** Jerk food is genuinely great, strong adults-only resort options, lively local music and nightlife
- **Cons:** Pricier than DR/Mexico, some resorts make it hard to leave the compound
- **Best time:** January–April for the best weather, or roll the dice on a cheaper late-Nov/early-Dec trip right as hurricane season tapers off
- **Book:** [Montego Bay vacations](https://www.cheapcaribbean.com/destinations/jamaica/montego-bay/) (CheapCaribbean)

### 4. Los Cabos, Mexico
- **Price:** ~$2,800–3,800/couple for 5 nights all-in
- **Pros:** Best food and design of the bunch, desert-meets-ocean scenery, great for pool days and a nice dinner at night
- **Cons:** Most expensive option here, longer flight (~6-7 hrs), water can be rough or cold some months
- **Best time:** Lower hurricane risk on the Pacific side, but it still peaks Aug–Oct — book Nov–May
- **Book:** [Best all-inclusive stays in Los Cabos](https://www.booking.com/all-inclusive/region/mx/los-cabos.html) (Booking.com)

## 🚢 Cruise (a few stops instead of one, less to coordinate day-to-day)

### 1. Caribbean round-trip ex-NYC/Cape Liberty
- **Price:** ~$1,100–1,900/couple, 7 nights, balcony cabin
- **Pros:** Depending where you're coming from, you might skip flying entirely and just drive or train to the port. Otherwise it's a short, often-cheap flight into Newark. Carnival/NCL also sail this route.
- **Cons:** First 2 sea days are colder before you hit warm water, ships on this route skew slightly less luxe
- **Best time:** Spring (Apr–May) or late fall (Nov) — warm enough in the Caribbean, mild enough at home
- **Book:** [Cruises from Cape Liberty (New York)](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/cruise-from/new-york-new-jersey) (Royal Caribbean)

### 2. Eastern/Western Caribbean ex-Miami or Fort Lauderdale
- **Price:** ~$1,400–2,200/couple, 7 nights, balcony cabin
- **Pros:** Biggest ship selection (Royal Caribbean, Celebrity), most island stops, best pool deck scene for our age group
- **Cons:** Everyone flies to Miami/FLL first — an extra travel day and cost on top of the cruise fare
- **Best time:** Dec–April for calm seas and sunshine; cheaper June–Nov if you don't mind some hurricane-season risk
- **Book:** [Cruises from Florida](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/florida-cruises) (Royal Caribbean)

## ✈️ Destination Trip (one home base, more independence/DIY)

### 1. Tulum villa or boutique hotel, Mexico
- **Price:** ~$1,600–2,400/couple for 5 nights (splitting a villa 4-6 ways can push this down a lot)
- **Pros:** Feels more like a real trip than a resort — restaurants, beach clubs, day trips to ruins and cenotes — without being hard to plan
- **Cons:** More day-to-day logistics than a resort (everyone has to agree on dinner, transport, etc.)
- **Best time:** Same as resort Tulum — Dec–April is postcard weather, so book before hurricane season (Jun–Nov) makes it a gamble
- **Book:** [Villa rentals near Tulum Beach](https://www.vrbo.com/vacation-rentals/villas/mexico/tulum/tulum-beach) (Vrbo)

### 2. Lisbon + Algarve, Portugal
- **Price:** ~$1,500–2,200/couple for 7 nights, lodging only, split between the city and the coast
- **Pros:** Great food/wine scene, strong value vs. Western Europe, walkable cities, beach time in the Algarve
- **Cons:** Longest flight (7–8.5 hrs) and pricier to get to (~$700–1,100/couple round-trip), bigger time-zone adjustment
- **Best time:** May–June or September — warm enough to swim in the Algarve, not yet packed with August crowds
- **Book:** [Portugal holidays](https://www.expedia.co.uk/Portugal.d147.Holidays-City-Breaks) (Expedia — covers both Lisbon and the Algarve)

## Recommendation if you want to steer the group

If low-coordination is the priority: **Punta Cana or Riviera Maya** is the easy, safe pick — good price, short flight, nothing to plan once it's booked. The **NYC-departure cruise** is the fun wildcard if people are cruise-curious, and depending where everyone's coming from, some of us might not even need to fly.

Toss both into the planner and let the group vote on dates + budget — that'll probably knock out a couple of these on its own.

## Before You Book — Things the Headline Prices Don't Cover

- **["All-inclusive" and "cruise fare" both have exclusions.](https://all-inclusive-guide.com/guides/what-is-all-inclusive/)** Cruise prices above are cabin fare only — add gratuities (~$18–20/person/day, usually auto-charged), a drink package if anyone wants one ($70–100+/day/person), and shore excursions. Resort "all-inclusive" often excludes top-shelf liquor, spa, some excursions, and resort/tourism taxes (sometimes $20–50/night, charged at checkout, not shown at booking). Budget **$500–1,000+/couple on top of the headline number**.
- 🧳 **Remember travel insurance!** Locking in a date over a year out means plans can change — a $100–200/couple cancellation policy protects your deposit.
- 🛂 **Remember to check passport validity!** Needs 6+ months left past your travel date — DR enforces it, but Mexico/Jamaica airlines sometimes do too, so it's the safe bar for everyone. Plenty of time to renew since it's 2027, just don't let it sneak up on you.
